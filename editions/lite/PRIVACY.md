# AI Hint Lite Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 23, 2026**

AI Hint Lite reads the text you select only after you invoke the extension. The selected text and follow-up prompts are sent to Chrome's built-in on-device AI for a response. AI Hint Lite does not connect to third-party AI providers or developer-operated servers.

The extension stores response preferences, the offline game's high score, and the most recent 40 prompts and answers in Chrome extension-local storage. You can search, copy, delete individual history items, or clear the history. Lite can also read up to the configured text limit from the main content of a page when you explicitly choose “Кратко о странице”. The extension does not store API keys, sync this information, or include analytics, advertising, or telemetry. Removing the extension or clearing its data removes the locally stored settings, history, and score.

Chrome's on-device model may need to be downloaded by Chrome. Chrome platform data practices apply to that model download and use.

AI Hint Lite requires only the Chrome permissions needed to read an explicitly selected text fragment, run the extension when invoked, save settings, provide its keyboard shortcut, and add the selection context-menu action.

For privacy questions, open a GitHub issue in the [AI Hint repository](https://github.com/Williamssdkok/ai-hint-chrome-extension/issues).
